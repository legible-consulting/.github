# Contributing

Client repositories accept pull requests from invited collaborators only. The client repo's
`CLAUDE.md` and `.icm/intake/CONTEXT.md` are the whole job. Branch, open a small PR, keep
commits in Conventional Commits form. CI and Vercel post the checks and a preview URL. Jamie
merges. Nobody pushes to `main`.

Public repositories (`blueprint`, this one) take issues and PRs from anyone.
